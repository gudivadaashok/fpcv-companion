The gradient

In 2D the derivative is a vector, ∇I = (∂I/∂x, ∂I/∂y). Its length is the edge strength, and it points across the edge toward the brighter side. Click the strength image to see the vector at that pixel.

Laplacian of Gaussian

The Laplacian ∇²I = ∂²I/∂x² + ∂²I/∂y² is the 2D second derivative. It does not depend on direction, and edges are where it crosses zero. Larger σ finds only coarse edges.
