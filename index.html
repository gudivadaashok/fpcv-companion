Vision Bench

Interactive demos for the core ideas of computer vision. Click sample images or upload your own, move the sliders, and watch each algorithm work step by step.

A companion to Shree Nayar's lecture series First Principles of Computer Vision. Watch a lecture, then try the idea here. Everything runs in your browser.

Imaging · Binary Images

Binary Images

Thresholding, connected-component labeling, and each object's area, centroid, orientation and roundedness. Includes a step-by-step labeling animation.
Imaging · Image Processing I & II

Image Processing

Point operations and histogram equalization, convolution with an editable kernel, Gaussian vs median vs bilateral filtering, and Fourier filtering.
Features · Edge Detection

Edge Detection

Derivatives along one row, gradient operators, Canny step by step (non-maximum suppression and hysteresis), and Laplacian zero crossings.
Reconstruction I · Photometric Stereo

Photometric Stereo

Drag three lights, recover normals and albedo, see the reflectance-map curves meet in gradient space, and rotate the recovered 3D shape.
Reconstruction II · Optical Flow

Optical Flow

Lucas–Kanade with a coarse-to-fine pyramid, error measured against the true motion, and the aperture problem shown as constraint lines.
