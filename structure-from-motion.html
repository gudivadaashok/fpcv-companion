Structure from Motion, Interactively

Track a few points through a video and recover both the 3D shape of the object and how the camera moved. Tomasi–Kanade factorization does it with one matrix decomposition.

The scene is synthetic, so the true shape is known and the recovered shape can be scored. Follows the Structure from Motion lectures of First Principles of Computer Vision.

00

Choose an object and a camera path

An orthographic camera orbits the object, swinging left and right with a little nod and roll. Each frame, every point is projected to the image and perturbed by Gaussian noise.

01

The video and its tracked points

All the algorithm sees is where each point lands in every frame. Faint trails show the track so far. Click a point to follow it. Its full track is drawn in orange.

02

The measurement matrix and its rank

Stack the horizontal coordinates of all frames above the vertical coordinates, one column per point. Subtract each row's mean so the object's centroid sits at the origin. Without noise, this 2F × P matrix has rank 3, because it is a product of a 2F × 3 motion matrix and a 3 × P shape matrix.

Why rank 3

W̃ = M S, W̃ is 2F × P, M is 2F × 3, S is 3 × P

Row f of M holds the camera's horizontal axis if, and row F+f holds its vertical axis jf. Column p of S is the point's 3D position sp. Each measurement is a dot product: ufp − ūf = if · sp. Noise lifts the fourth and later singular values off zero. Keeping only three removes much of that noise.

03

Factorize, then make the motion a true rotation

The SVD gives a valid factorization, but any invertible 3 × 3 matrix Q can slip in between: M̂Ŝ = (M̂Q)(Q⁻¹Ŝ). The camera axes must be unit length and perpendicular, which pins Q down.

The two steps

1. W̃ = U Σ Vᵀ → M̂ = U₃ Σ₃^½, Ŝ = Σ₃^½ V₃ᵀ

2. m̂ₖᵀ L m̂ₖ = 1, n̂ₖᵀ L n̂ₖ = 1, m̂ₖᵀ L n̂ₖ = 0, L = Q Qᵀ

M = M̂ Q, S = Q⁻¹ Ŝ

The three constraints per frame are linear in the six unknowns of the symmetric matrix L. Stack them for all frames and solve by least squares. A Cholesky factorization L = Q Qᵀ then gives Q. With noise, L can lose positive definiteness. This page then clips its negative eigenvalues, which is a simplification. What remains unknown is a global rotation (and mirror) of the whole scene.

04

Recovered shape against the truth

The recovered shape lives in its own arbitrary frame. A Procrustes step finds the best rotation to line it up with the true shape. Drag to rotate the view, scroll or pinch to zoom. Click a point to select it.

Try this

Set noise to 0 and check that the error is almost exactly zero. Raise the noise to 2 px and watch the fourth singular value rise and the error grow. Then cut the frames to 4 and see how much worse noise hurts when there is less data to average. With few frames the camera barely moves, so depth is poorly determined.

Simplifications: the camera is orthographic (no perspective), every point is visible in every frame, and tracks are given, not detected.
